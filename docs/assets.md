# Assets and ownership

The template ships one photographic portrait style with light and dark sprites
in `public/assets/images/portrait.webp` and `portrait-dark.webp`.
It is Dani Asyrofi's demonstration identity, included at his request. Its use is
limited by the identity clause in LICENSE. Replace it before launching your site.

The geometric book covers and six project illustrations were authored for this
template as editable SVGs. They contain no client logos, stock photos, or copied
article artwork. Sample project descriptions and article text are starter content,
not claims about real clients or measured outcomes.

Apple and SpaceX company marks are separate assets with upstream notices in
`public/assets/images/companies/`. The other company marks are original fictional
identities. The appearance of a mark does not imply endorsement or employment.

Heroicons retains `public/assets/icons/HEROICONS-LICENSE.txt`. The Kugiri text
splitter retains `public/assets/folio/enhancements/vendor/LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md`.
Fonts are unmodified upstream binaries; their OFL notices are stored beside them.
Caveat remains inside the vendor directory with its OFL notice.

Design references are listed on the generated Credits page. The motion research
record in this repository explains which examples were source excerpts and which
were checked in a live build. References do not imply endorsement or a license
grant for someone else's site or imagery.

Earlier public source licensing is preserved in `licenses/previous-release.txt`.
The new custom license cannot withdraw rights already granted under MIT, and its
no-resale clause does not apply to independent use of third-party dependencies.
